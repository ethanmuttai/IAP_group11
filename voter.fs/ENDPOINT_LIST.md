Team 11: Voter Edu (Downstream API for Group 12)

|Method | Path | Purpose | Maps to Need|
|:--- |:---- |:--- |:--- |
|GET | /candidates | Gets all candidates — basic info plus their policy positions. | "AccessMap Nairobi needs to read the list of candidates and their policy positions, in order to display civic info on venue pages for voter education centers." |
|GET | /candidates/{candidateId} | One candidate's full profile: experience, policies, vision. | "AccessMap Nairobi needs to read a single candidate's full profile, in order to show detailed civic info when a venue page links out to that specific candidate." |
|GET | /candidates/{candidateId}/reviews | All reviews for a candidate (rating + description). | "AccessMap Nairobi needs to read reviews for a specific candidate in order to show community sentiment on venue pages hosting civic events." |
|POST | /candidates/{candidateId}/reviews | Adds a new review to a candidate. | Internal — for our own review feature (Reviews: Create, from the Week 1 audit). |
|PUT | /candidates/{candidateId}/reviews/{reviewId} | Edits an existing review by replacing it (rating + text; reviewer name and original date are kept). | Internal — for our own review feature (Reviews: Edit), so a voter can fix or update a review they posted. |
|GET | /candidates/{candidateId}/reviews?minRating={n} | Same as above but filtered by min star rating. | Internal — for our own review feature (Reviews: Filter, from the Week 1 audit). |
|GET | /rallies | Civic events and rallies: venue, venue coordinates, date, start and end time (Nairobi time) and expected attendance. Optional `?upcoming=true` returns only events that have not ended. | Provided for Group 12 (accessibility and traffic planning around event venues), in addition to candidates and reviews. Sample data. |
|GET | /rallies/{rallyId}/travel?from={landmarkId} | Travel advice for a civic event: fastest and cheapest ride from a landmark to the venue, crowd-pressure level, suggested leave-by time and an optional walkable alternative drop-off. | Internal — our own feature built on Group 10's landmarks and rides plus our rallies feed. Estimates only. |
|GET | /polling-stations | Our own list of polling stations, grouped by area, with approximate coordinates. A team sample, not the official IEBC register (see `verified`). | Internal — used by our polling station page. |
|GET | /polling-stations/{stationId}/nearest-landmark | The Group 10 landmark nearest to a polling station, so ride prices can be requested for it. | Internal — Group 10's rides only understand their own landmarks, so prices are for the landmark area, not the exact station. |
