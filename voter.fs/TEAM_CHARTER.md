 App Summary

Voter Edu is a website application that provides simplified and accurate information on the most recent presidential candidates. Users can access information such as experience, policies, and visions, and compare candidates side by side to see which one fits best for them. Users can also leave reviews on specific candidates. The main goal of the application is to provide voter education that is free from bias and simplified, for Kenyan citizens.
